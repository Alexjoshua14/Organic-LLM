# Remy

Remy is Organic LLM's cooking assistant. It helps you plan meals for the week, build a recipe library and make a shopping list, with a conversation always one click away.

## What it is for

- **This week's plan:** breakfast, lunch and dinner across the week, in one view
- **A library** of recipes you keep and reuse
- **A shopping list** that follows from the plan
- Planning an event, such as a dinner party, as its own one-off menu

## How it works

Remy has a desktop dashboard whose default view is the week. The conversation opens beside it as a dock, so you can ask for a swap and watch the plan change. Full-page threads are still available when you want to talk without the dashboard.

## How it differs from Chat

Chat answers a question. Remy keeps a plan, so what you decided on Monday is still in the week on Thursday.

## When to use it

Open Remy for anything with a plate at the end of it. Use main Chat for general questions.
